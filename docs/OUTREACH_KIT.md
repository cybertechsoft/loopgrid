# LoopGrid design-partner outreach kit

## Who to contact first

Prioritize teams that already have AI agents taking consequential actions: refunds/credits, claims, account changes, permissions, customer operations, financial workflows, healthcare operations or other state-changing workflows.

Best initial contacts: CTO, Head of AI/AI Platform, CISO/Security Engineering, AI Governance/Risk, or the engineering lead responsible for the agent workflow.

## 20-second verbal pitch

LoopGrid is the evidence plane for AI agents. Agent runtimes execute and observability explains, but when an agent takes a consequential action, teams still need a portable record proving who acted, under what authority and policy, what human oversight happened, what tool actually ran and what outcome followed. LoopGrid captures and cryptographically seals that evidence so it can be verified later without trusting the running application.

## Pilot success criteria

A pilot is successful when a real or controlled workflow can:

1. capture the consequential decision and its authority/context;
2. record deterministic policy provenance;
3. record human review where required;
4. record external action and observed outcome;
5. reach complete evidence coverage;
6. export a portable evidence bundle;
7. verify the bundle independently with signer identity pinned out-of-band;
8. document the remaining production-specific gaps for that customer environment.
