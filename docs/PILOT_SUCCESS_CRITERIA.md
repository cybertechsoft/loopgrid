# Design-Partner Pilot Success Criteria

A useful LoopGrid pilot starts with **one consequential AI/agent workflow**, not a broad platform rollout.

A pilot is successful when:

- the partner identifies one state-changing or customer-impacting decision;
- LoopGrid captures agent identity, authority, model/context and policy provenance;
- required human oversight is evidenced when applicable;
- the actual tool/action and observed outcome are recorded;
- evidence coverage reaches the expected complete state;
- workspace verification passes and a deliberate tamper simulation is detected;
- a portable evidence bundle verifies offline with signer pinning;
- the partner can explain at least one operational, audit, dispute or governance workflow that becomes materially easier with the evidence.

Commercial validation should additionally answer: who owns the problem, how often consequential decisions occur, required retention/deployment posture, and willingness to pay for managed evidence infrastructure.
